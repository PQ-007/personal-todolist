Privacy policy

Calpis · last updated October 25, 2026

Calpis is a personal weekly planner. It keeps your tasks as events in your own Google Calendar. This page explains what the app reads, where things are stored and how to remove them.

What the app accesses

When you connect Google Calendar, the app asks for one permission: calendar.events, to see and edit events in your calendars. It uses it only to:

  • show the events of the calendar you choose (your main calendar unless you pick another) for the days on screen;
  • create, change, move, mark done and delete tasks when you do that in the app.

It doesn't read your contacts, email, files or any other Google data, and it doesn't access other calendars than the one you choose.

Where your data is stored

  • Your tasks stay in your Google Calendar. The app shows them in your browser; it doesn't copy them to any server or database.
  • Sign-in. To keep you signed in, the app's sign-in function (hosted on Netlify) receives a Google refresh token and stores it in a secure, httpOnly cookie in your own browser. The function doesn't keep it anywhere else. It uses it only to get short-lived access tokens for your calendar.
  • Settings, the to-do stack and demo data are kept in your browser's local storage on your device.

What the app doesn't do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party cookies.
  • Your data isn't sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and isn't used for any purpose other than showing and editing your planner.
  • No person reads your calendar data, and it isn't used to train AI models.

Calpis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Settings → Sign out in the app revokes its access and deletes the sign-in cookie.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Clearing the site's data in your browser removes the settings and stack stored on your device.
  • Tasks you created are ordinary Google Calendar events; delete them in the app or in Google Calendar.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
